## Question 1: Which 5-man lineup in the league has the most positive on-court impact?

### Answer

The 5-man lineup with the most positive on-court impact is **Foghorn Leghorn, Tweety, LeBron James, Elmer Fudd, and Bugs Bunny** from the **Tune Squad**, with a **Net Rating of +122.2** over **18 total possessions** and a **+11 plus/minus**.

### Methodology

To determine on-court impact, I used **Net Rating** (Offensive Rating − Defensive Rating) as the primary metric, where Offensive Rating measures points scored per 100 possessions and Defensive Rating measures points allowed per 100 possessions. A positive net rating indicates the lineup outscores opponents while on the floor.

While several lineups posted extremely high net ratings (e.g., +300.0, +200.0), those were based on very small sample sizes (1–5 possessions), making them statistically unreliable. The lineup above was chosen because it represents the best balance of a strong net rating and a meaningful sample size (18 possessions, the highest among top-tier net rating lineups with ≥12 possessions).

This lineup's offensive rating of approximately **161.1** points per 100 possessions is elite, while its defensive rating of approximately **38.9** demonstrates dominant defensive performance — holding opponents to far below average scoring efficiency.

## Question 3: Which 5-man lineup is best suited to countering a rebounding-heavy big lineup?

### Answer

The best 5-man lineup to counter a rebounding-heavy big lineup is **Nightwing, Phantom Stranger, Wonder Woman, Black Canary, and Batman** from the **Justice League**.

### Methodology & Rationale

To counter a team that deploys oversized lineups emphasizing offensive and defensive rebounding, the ideal counter-strategy focuses on three pillars:

1. **Defensive Rebounding (deny second chances):** The most critical counter to a glass-crashing team is securing defensive rebounds to prevent second-chance points. This lineup has a **100% defensive rebound rate (DREB%)** — they converted every defensive rebound opportunity, giving the opposing offense zero second chances.

2. **Three-Point Shooting (stretch the floor):** Against large, rim-protecting lineups, perimeter shooting pulls big defenders away from the basket, reducing their rebounding advantage and creating driving lanes. This lineup averages **0.5 three-pointers made per offensive possession**, demonstrating a strong ability to generate and convert perimeter looks.

3. **Shooting Efficiency (make size irrelevant):** By converting at a high rate, this lineup reduces the total number of missed shots (and therefore rebound opportunities) for the opponent. Their **Effective FG% is exceptional**, meaning fewer missed shots for opponents to crash the glass on.

Additional factors that make this lineup effective against size-heavy opponents:
- **Net Rating of +120.0** over 9 possessions shows dominant overall performance
- The combination of floor spacing and defensive rebounding discipline directly neutralizes the opponent's primary advantage
- Limiting opponent second-chance opportunities is the single most effective way to negate a rebounding-focused strategy, because even elite offensive rebounders cannot grab boards that don't exist (i.e., on made shots or secured defensive rebounds)

In essence, this Justice League lineup beats a big, rebounding-heavy team by **making shots at a high clip** (reducing opponent rebound opportunities), **spacing the floor with three-point shooting** (pulling bigs out of position), and **securing every defensive rebound** (denying second chances).
